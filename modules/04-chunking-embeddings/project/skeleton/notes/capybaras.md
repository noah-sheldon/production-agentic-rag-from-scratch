# Capybaras

## The animal

The capybara is the world's biggest rodent. It looks like a giant guinea pig,
with a heavy body, short legs, and a blunt head. It is a calm animal that
lives in groups near water.

## Where it lives

Capybaras live in South America, along rivers and in flooded grasslands.
They are strong swimmers and can stay underwater for minutes to hide from
predators.

## Why I care

A water-loving giant rodent is the perfect test case for the semantic index:
a query about "large rodent that swims" must find this note even when the
exact words differ.
